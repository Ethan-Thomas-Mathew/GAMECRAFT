Where the numbers come from

Ball.kt
Gravity 1.5 and flap impulse -25, applied once per step.
Pipe.kt
Pipes are 200 px wide. Hit boxes are rectangles, the same as here.
GameView.kt
A new pipe spawns once the last one has moved 55% of the screen width. The gap sits between 200 px from the top and 200 px from the bottom.
Difficulty
Easy 700 px gap at speed 7, Medium 600 at 8, Hard 500 at 10. Custom ranges: speed 5 to 15, gap 450 to 800, ball 30 to 70.
SharedPreferences
The app saves the high score and settings on the phone. This page saves them in your browser.

One difference: the Android version moves things once per drawn frame, so its speed depends on the phone's refresh rate. This page runs the game at a fixed 60 steps per second, so it plays the same on every screen.
